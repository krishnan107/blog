## A Celestial Treat

If you looked up at the night sky over the United Kingdom recently, you might have noticed something truly spectacular. Today is July 1, 2026, and we are still buzzing from the incredible display we witnessed last night. The first full Moon of the summer, affectionately known as the Strawberry Moon, graced our skies with a brilliant, golden glow that left amateur photographers and casual observers alike reaching for their cameras.

There is something inherently magical about a full Moon, but the Strawberry Moon feels special. It marks the transition into the heart of summer. While it does not always appear pink or red, the name originates from the tradition of harvesting wild strawberries during this time of year. Last night, the lunar surface appeared exceptionally crisp and bright, casting long shadows across the British landscape and providing a perfect backdrop for late evening walks.

## The Art of Moon Photography

Social media has been flooded with breathtaking imagery since the sun went down. From the iconic silhouette of the London skyline to the rugged, misty hills of the Scottish Highlands, the photos are nothing short of professional grade. Seeing people from all corners of the country sharing their perspectives on the same celestial event serves as a wonderful reminder of how we are all connected under the same vast sky.

Capturing the Moon is notoriously tricky. It requires a steady hand, the right lens, and a bit of luck with the cloud cover. Many of the photos circulating online show the Moon glowing behind historic landmarks, creating a beautiful contrast between human history and the timeless nature of our lunar neighbor. It is inspiring to see so many people taking a moment to slow down and appreciate the natural beauty occurring right above our heads.

## Why We Look Up

In our busy lives, it is easy to forget that we live on a planet spinning through a vast and mysterious universe. Events like the Strawberry Moon act as a gentle nudge to pause. Whether you spent the evening sitting in your garden with a cup of tea or ventured out to a dark sky park to get a clearer view, the experience was a shared highlight for the entire nation. 

If you missed the peak of the lunar glow, do not worry. Even though the official peak has passed, the Moon will remain bright and full for the next few nights. It is a fantastic excuse to step outside, breathe in the summer air, and spend a few minutes simply enjoying the view. The universe has a way of putting on a show that costs nothing to attend, and last night was a perfect example of that. 

We hope you managed to catch a glimpse of the Strawberry Moon last night. It was a beautiful start to July and a wonderful way to welcome the summer season. Keep your eyes on the horizon, because there is always something interesting happening in our night sky.
