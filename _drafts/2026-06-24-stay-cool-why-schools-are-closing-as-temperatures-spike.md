## A Scorching Start to Summer

It is June 24, 2026, and if you have stepped outside today, you know exactly what everyone is talking about. The heat is not just a passing inconvenience. It is a full scale weather event. Yesterday, Wisley in England hit a staggering 34.6 degrees Celsius. Meanwhile, our friends in Scotland and Northern Ireland are recording their hottest days of the entire year. It feels like the whole country is trapped in a giant oven, and as a result, the rhythm of daily life is shifting rapidly.

## Why The Schools Are Shutting Down

The biggest news hitting headlines this morning is the wave of school closures. Hundreds of institutions across the region have made the difficult decision to close their doors ahead of incoming red heat alerts. You might be wondering why we are shutting down schools when things have been warmer in other parts of the world. The reality is that our infrastructure was simply not built for this kind of sustained, intense heat. Many of our older school buildings act like greenhouses. They trap heat, lack advanced climate control, and can quickly become unsafe for students and staff alike.

Safety is the top priority for administrators. When classrooms become stifling, it is nearly impossible for students to focus or stay healthy. By closing early, schools are preventing the risk of heat exhaustion and ensuring that everyone stays in a cool environment while the mercury climbs.

## Keeping Your Cool During The Red Alert

With red heat alerts looming, it is time to take precautions. If you are heading out, remember that the sun is particularly unforgiving right now. Try to stay indoors during the peak hours of midday. If you must go out, carry plenty of water, wear light and breathable clothing, and seek out shade whenever possible. Do not forget about your neighbors, especially the elderly or those living in top floor apartments that tend to absorb the most heat. A quick check in can go a long way.

It is also worth noting that our local transit services may face delays as tracks expand in the heat. Check your travel plans before you leave the house and keep an eye on the official weather services for real time updates. We have seen temperatures rise before, but the speed at which this heatwave has arrived is a stark reminder of how much our climate is changing. While it is tempting to head to the nearest park for a picnic, today is definitely a day for staying low and keeping hydrated.

## Final Thoughts

We are all hoping for a bit of relief as the week progresses, but for now, the message is clear. Prioritize your health, stay hydrated, and keep a close eye on the official warnings. We will get through this heatwave together, but please do not take any unnecessary risks while the temperature remains at these record highs.
