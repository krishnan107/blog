## A Seasonal Spectacle

Happy Sunday, everyone! As we settle into the final weekend of June 2026, there is something extra special waiting for us in the night sky. If you have been enjoying the longer days and warmer evenings, get ready for a celestial treat because the full Strawberry Moon is set to grace our view this week.

While the name might make you think the moon will actually turn the color of a juicy piece of fruit, it is a bit more symbolic than that. The name historically comes from the Algonquin tribes who associated this specific full moon with the peak of the strawberry harvest season in North America. Regardless of its hue, it remains one of the most anticipated lunar events of the summer calendar.

## Why This Moon Matters

This particular moon is significant because it marks the first full moon of the summer season. After the summer solstice passed just over a week ago, we are officially deep in the heart of the year. Seeing the full moon hanging low on the horizon is always a stunning reminder of the Earths position in our solar system. Because it occurs during the summer months, the moon often appears to take a lower trajectory across the sky in the Northern Hemisphere. This can sometimes create a beautiful, golden, or even amber glow as the light filters through more of the atmosphere.

## How to Catch the Best View

You do not need any fancy telescopes or expensive equipment to enjoy this event. As long as the clouds stay away, the moon will be bright enough to see from your backyard, a local park, or even just through your bedroom window. If you want the best experience, try to head outside shortly after moonrise. When the moon is close to the horizon, it often creates a visual trick known as the moon illusion, where it appears much larger than it does when it is high overhead.

Find a spot with a clear view of the eastern horizon and keep an eye on the weather forecast. Even if there is a bit of cloud cover, the moon is typically bright enough to pierce through light mist or thin clouds, creating a lovely atmospheric halo effect. It is the perfect excuse to step away from your screens, head outdoors, and enjoy a quiet moment under the stars.

## Making the Most of the Night

Whether you are an avid stargazer or just someone who appreciates a beautiful night, take a moment to look up this week. Events like this are a great reminder to slow down and connect with the natural world. Grab a blanket, maybe a thermos of something warm, and enjoy the show that nature has provided for us.

We hope you have a clear view and a wonderful time watching the Strawberry Moon rise. It is the perfect way to kick off the summer season and appreciate the beauty of our night sky.
