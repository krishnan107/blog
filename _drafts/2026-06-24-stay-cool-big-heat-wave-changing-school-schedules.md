## A Scorching Start to Summer

If you have stepped outside today, June 24, 2026, you already know exactly what everyone is talking about. It is undeniably hot. While we are used to the typical British summer fluctuations, today feels different. Yesterday, the mercury hit 34.6 degrees Celsius in Wisley, England, and the heat has clearly not slowed down as we move through the week.

It is not just England feeling the burn. Scotland and Northern Ireland have both recorded their hottest days of the year so far. For many of us, this means reaching for the extra bottle of water and finding the nearest patch of shade. But for parents and students, this weather has triggered a much more logistical headache.

## Classrooms Closing Their Doors

In response to the red heat alerts currently in effect, hundreds of schools across the country have made the difficult decision to close their doors. It is a tough call for administrators to make. On one hand, education is vital. On the other hand, keeping children in classrooms that were not designed for these record breaking temperatures is simply not safe or productive.

Many of these older school buildings act like greenhouses once the sun starts beating down. Without adequate air conditioning, temperatures inside can quickly climb even higher than the readings we see on official thermometers. Teachers are reporting that students are struggling to focus, and the risk of heat exhaustion is becoming a genuine concern for local health authorities.

## How to Handle the Heat

If your local school has moved to remote learning or closed for the day, the best thing you can do is keep things simple. Stay indoors during the peak hours of the afternoon if you can. If you must go out, wear light clothing and keep that water bottle glued to your hand. It is easy to underestimate how quickly dehydration can set in when the air feels this heavy.

We are all hoping for a bit of a breeze and a return to more manageable temperatures soon. Until then, check your local school district website or social media channels for the latest updates on closures. It is better to be safe and stay cool while we wait for this heat wave to break.

## Looking Ahead

While the heat is intense right now, these extreme weather events serve as a reminder of how quickly our environment can shift. Take care of your neighbors, check on the elderly, and keep your pets cool. We will get through this week together, and hopefully, the cooler weather is just around the corner.
