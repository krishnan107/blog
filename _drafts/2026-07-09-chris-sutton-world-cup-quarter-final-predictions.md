## The World Cup fever is peaking

It is July 9, 2026, and the atmosphere across the globe is absolutely electric. We are deep into the business end of the World Cup, and the tension is palpable. The group stages are a distant memory, and the round of 16 provided enough drama to last a lifetime. Now, we are down to the final eight teams. Everyone has an opinion on who will lift the trophy, but there is one man whose predictions always spark a debate: Chris Sutton.

## Sutton weighs in on the big ties

As always, BBC Sport's resident expert Chris Sutton has stuck his neck out with his latest round of predictions. He has looked at the remaining fixtures and given us his take on who will advance to the semi-finals. Sutton is never one to shy away from a bold claim, and this time is no different. He has analyzed the tactical setups, the individual form of the stars, and the sheer grit required to win at this stage of the tournament.

## The England vs Norway spotlight

The tie that has everyone talking is undoubtedly England against Norway. It is a massive clash between a Three Lions side that looks increasingly settled and a Norway squad powered by some of the most exciting talent in the world. Sutton points out that England has managed to navigate the pressure well so far, but he warns that Norway possesses a lethal edge that could cause serious problems for the English back line. His breakdown of how the midfield battle will likely unfold is particularly insightful. He thinks it will be a tight, cagey affair that could potentially go all the way to penalties. If England wants to progress, he argues, they must find a way to neutralize the threat on the counterattack.

## Why we love the predictions

What makes Sutton so engaging is his willingness to be wrong. He does not hide behind safe answers. Whether you agree with his assessment or think he is completely off the mark, his analysis forces us to look at these games through a different lens. It reminds us that at this level, the margins between victory and defeat are incredibly thin. One tactical tweak or a single moment of brilliance can change everything. As fans, we love to debate these points, check the stats, and imagine how our favorite teams will fare under the spotlight of the world stage.

## Looking ahead to the weekend

As we head into these quarter-final matches, the excitement is reaching a fever pitch. Will Sutton be right about England, or will Norway pull off the shock of the tournament? We will have to wait and see how it all plays out on the pitch. Regardless of the final results, one thing is certain: we are in for a weekend of football that we will remember for a long time to come.
