## 3. Thai Princess Bajrakitiyabha dies after more than three years in coma

Thailand is in mourning today following the announcement that Princess Bajrakitiyabha has passed away. The eldest daughter of the king had been in a coma since December 2022, when she collapsed while exercising her dogs. Her death marks the end of a long and painful period of uncertainty for the royal family and the nation at large.

As a prominent member of the royal family, the Princess was widely respected and her health struggles were a matter of national concern for years. The government has declared a period of mourning as citizens gather to pay their respects. Her passing is a profound loss for the monarchy, coming at a time when the institution is already navigating a complex political environment within the country.
