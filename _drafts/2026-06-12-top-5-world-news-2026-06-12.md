## 1. Tehran says nothing finalised after Trump claims deal to end Iran war near

Confusion reigns in the Middle East today after United States President Donald Trump announced that a great settlement had been reached to bring an end to the ongoing conflict with Iran. The president spoke to reporters from the White House, suggesting that a breakthrough was imminent and that the terms were nearly finalized. His optimism appeared to signal a potential cooling of tensions that have gripped the region for months.

However, officials in Tehran have quickly moved to dampen these expectations, labeling the reports of a deal as purely speculative. This stark disconnect between the two capitals highlights the ongoing volatility and lack of a unified diplomatic framework.

Regardless of the intent, the result is a precarious diplomatic environment where the line between negotiation and propaganda remains frustratingly blurred.

## 3. Thai Princess Bajrakitiyabha dies after more than three years in coma

The Royal Palace of Thailand has officially announced the passing of Princess Bajrakitiyabha, the eldest daughter of King Vajiralongkorn. The Princess had been in a coma since December 2022, when she collapsed suddenly while exercising her dogs at a military facility. Her death marks the end of a long period of uncertainty for the Thai monarchy and the nation at large.

For more than three years, the Princess remained under constant medical care as the nation waited for updates on her condition. Her death is expected to trigger a period of national mourning and may have implications for the succession plans of the royal family. She was widely viewed as a prominent and capable figure within the institution, and her loss is being felt deeply across the country.

Tensions in East Jerusalem have reached a breaking point following a significant increase in the demolition of Palestinian homes. For those who have lost their homes, the impact is devastating. Families describe the demolitions as a calculated effort to erase their history and deny them a future in the city. As the international community watches these developments, the situation serves as a stark reminder of the enduring conflict over land and sovereignty in one of the most contested regions on earth. The anger on the ground is palpable, with many fearing that more demolitions are planned for the coming months.
