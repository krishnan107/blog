The world has plenty to talk about today — from Lionel Messi's magical return on the pitch to a major immigration bill clearing the U.S. House.

## ⚽ Messi Scores as Argentina Preps for World Cup

In what may be his final World Cup warm-up ever, Lionel Messi came off the bench in the 70th minute against Iceland and immediately made his mark. His first touch drew a foul, and he stepped up to bury the penalty — his **117th international goal** — as Argentina cruised to a 3-0 win in Auburn, Alabama.

Valentin Barco and Thiago Almada also got on the scoresheet. Argentina head into the 2026 FIFA World Cup, which officially kicks off June 11 on home soil across the United States, Canada, and Mexico.

## 🇺🇸 House Passes $70 Billion Immigration Package

The U.S. House passed a sweeping immigration enforcement bill Tuesday by a razor-thin 214–212 margin. The $70 billion package locks in ICE funding through the end of the Trump presidency. Democrats strongly opposed the bill — it now heads to the Senate.

## 🗳️ California Governor's Race Set

Democrat **Javier Becerra** and Trump-backed Republican **Steve Hilton** will face off in November for California governor after Tuesday's jungle primary.

## 🏀 Trump at the NBA Finals

President Trump attended Game 3 of the NBA Finals at Madison Square Garden — Knicks vs. Spurs — and received a mixed reaction from the sold-out crowd.

## 🇬🇧 Belfast Protests After Stabbing

UK leaders called for calm after anti-immigration protests erupted in Belfast following the arrest of a Sudanese man accused of a violent stabbing.
