Today is the day. The FIFA World Cup 2026 has officially arrived.

From the electric opening ceremony at Estadio Azteca to England's sharp warm-up win, the football world is buzzing. Here is everything that happened on Day One of the biggest World Cup in history.

## Shakira Owns the Azteca

Before a ball was kicked, Shakira owned the night.

The Colombian superstar headlined the opening ceremony at Mexico City's legendary Estadio Azteca. Tonight it witnessed a 16-minute spectacle of music, colour, and culture that announced this tournament to the world.

Shakira performed **Dai Dai**, the official song of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, alongside Nigerian star Burna Boy -- their first live performance before a global audience of hundreds of millions. The stage also featured Belinda, Danny Ocean, J Balvin, Tyla, and a special appearance by Hollywood actress Salma Hayek Pinault as FIFA's tournament ambassador.

Loud, proud, and unmistakably Latin American.

## Mexico vs. South Africa -- History Repeats

Then came the match: **Mexico vs. South Africa** in the tournament opener.

The storyline was irresistible. Exactly 16 years ago, on June 11, 2010, South Africa opened the World Cup against Mexico in Johannesburg -- and the Bafana Bafana held El Tri to a 1-1 draw in front of their home nation. Tonight the two sides met again in a World Cup opener, this time with Mexico as co-host on home soil at Azteca.

The roar of 87,000 fans inside the stadium was deafening. For Mexico, playing a home World Cup carries enormous pressure. For South Africa, it was a chance to write another chapter in a rivalry that began with one of football's most memorable opening games.

Group A also includes **South Korea** and **Czechia**, making it one of the more intriguing groups of the tournament.

## England Arrive in Style

Elsewhere, **England warmed up with a commanding 3-0 win over Costa Rica** in Florida.

Declan Rice opened the scoring early on his return to the squad. Anthony Gordon added a penalty in the second half, and Ollie Watkins completed the rout with a composed finish. The Three Lions looked sharp, organised, and hungry -- precisely what manager Gareth Southgate would have wanted ahead of the group stage.

For England fans the question is familiar: is this finally the year?

## The Biggest World Cup Ever

The 2026 edition is unlike anything football has seen before:

- **48 nations** competing for the first time (up from 32)
- **104 matches** across three host countries
- **United States, Mexico, and Canada** sharing hosting duties -- the first tri-nation World Cup in history
- Iconic venues from MetLife Stadium in New Jersey to SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles to the Azteca itself

The expanded format means more teams, more upsets, and more stories. A nation that has never reached a quarter-final could go deep this year.
